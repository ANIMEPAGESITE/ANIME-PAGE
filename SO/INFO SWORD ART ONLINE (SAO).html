Sword Art Online: Progressive

Thoughts

Personally, I think that this anime truly proves the meaning of power of love.The reason is that his love for her was that much that it broke the system itself.
Anyways his strugle to save her was really heart warming.He took a life worth of risk for her by entering in a similer game which almost had them killed.
The anime is great because it some how manages to provide both a love story and action with adventure without making it senseless at any point.
It truly amazes me. So yeah, these were my thoughts about this masterpiece.
